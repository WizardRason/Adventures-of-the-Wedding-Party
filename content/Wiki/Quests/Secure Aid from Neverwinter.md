# Premise
The [[Wiki/Organizations/Lords' Alliance]] want us to secure funding for sending aid to [[Wiki/Places/Phandalin]]. There is a vault in the Blue Lake District controlled by [[Wiki/People/Neverwinter/Victor\|Victor Vidorant]], the leader of a pick pocket group in [[Wiki/Places/Neverwinter]]. They want us to rob their vault for the funding.
They want us to secure a few thousand gold to fund sending a dozen or so troops sent out. 
One of the ex guards from them can be found at the Beached Leviathan
