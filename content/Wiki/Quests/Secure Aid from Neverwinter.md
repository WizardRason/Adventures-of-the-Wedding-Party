# Premise

The [[Lords' Alliance]] want us to secure funding for sending aid to [[Phandalin]]. There is a vault in the Blue Lake District controlled by [[Victor|Victor Vidorant]], the leader of a pick pocket group in [[Neverwinter]]. They want us to rob their vault for the funding.
They want us to secure a few thousand gold to fund sending a dozen or so troops sent out.
One of the ex guards from them can be found at the Beached Leviathan
