# Details

The original Town Master of [[Phandalin]]. He was pressured by the [[Redbrands]] when they had power in the town, and was very thankful when they were defeated and removed. Overall a coward, and barely willing to leave his own home.

Was threatened in the middle of the night by [[Vinmyr]] at the behest of [[Halia]] to leave [[Phandalin]]. The next morning there was disarray as he did just that, and disappeared from the town, leading directly to the events of the [[Phandalin Election]]
