# Premise

The local midwife- an acolyte of Chauntea named Adabra Gwynn- lives by herself in a stone windmill on the side of a hill a few miles south of [[Phandalin]]. With dragon sightings becoming common, it's not safe for her to be alone. Urge Adabra to return to Phandalin. Once she's safe, visit
Townmaster Harbin Wester to claim a reward of 25 gp.
