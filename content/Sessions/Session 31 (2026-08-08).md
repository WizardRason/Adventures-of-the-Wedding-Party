Continuing towards [[Neverwinter]] Woods, and continue traveling until night.

In the middle of the night, while [[Doe]] and [[Valerian]] are keeping watch, the [[Wedding Party|Party]] is ambushed by a group of bugbears and goblins. They are being led by a familiar face, Yeemik from [[Session 01 (2025-02-15)]]. [[Morgan]] is brought down, and [[Valerian]] becomes possessed.

[[Doe]] is able to bring about lightning strikes, and brings down some of the fleeing and hiding enemies, including Yeemik.

The rest of the assailants are slowly chipped away, removing them as threats and preventing them from stealing people away. The last of the enemies have been brought down, and now [[Valerian]] is being confronted about his curse.

---

Spencer the spider continues to lead the [[Wedding Party|Party]] through [[Neverwinter]] Woods, reaching a huge tree stump, that looks as if it once towered above the rest of the woods.

Inside the silken sanctuary, we find a red skinned tiefling woman, with a horns, a whipping tail, and one red and one purple eye. [[Wedelia]] tells her that [[Lyriana]] sent us for Pompom's sake. The Tiefling introduces herself as [[Larissa]]. She expounds on the Silken Sanctuary and what it is. It is where the spiders journey for first and last rights from the first spider.

Donations were made to [[Larissa]] to remove the cursed axe from [[Valerian]]. [[Larissa]] leads the party to an entrance to the shrine, and the [[Wedding Party|Party]] begins heading down.
