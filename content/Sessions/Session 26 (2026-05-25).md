[[Wedelia]] collapsed before the combat begins, succumbing to a infected sturge attack

The undead dwarf castelin calls out for his children, and ghouls begin entering through corners of the room

More ghouls arrive from adjacent rooms, drawn by the loud noises.

[[Vinmyr]] strikes the dwarf, and he screams out, powering up the nearby ghouls

Plant growth is cast around the entire dungeon, and is caught on fire. That fire is spreading, making a hazardous environment for everyone

The dwarf is finally struck down, and partially looted.

The [[Wedding Party]] waits for the flames to die down, and does a little exploring of the rooms off the throne room.

[[Valerian]] explores upstairs, and found the ghostly visage of Vildara weeping with her back turned. He tells her the castelin was dead, and she said to go to the fireplace in his room.

Continued searching around in these rooms, we find a couple magical item.

All the ghouls were destroyed, Vildara was appeased, and attempt to befriend the spiders above were made. The attempts succeeded, and the leader [[Lyriana]] will stay friendly so long as they are supplied good berries each day.

We explore the remaining rooms, now that it is safe. Magic items are identified and the working ballista is disassembled poorly, but with some help it could be put back together successfully
