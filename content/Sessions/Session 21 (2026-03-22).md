# Knowledge Gained

## [[Doe]] - Global Catastrophes

Was caused by the awakening of a large fire elemental (Megera)
Decimated the woods and surrounding area
Survivors were ferried to [[UPMC]]

## [[Vinmyr]] - Drow History

Finds notes of a house [[Lumaday]], which translates to light shadow
Stood out because they did not worship Lothe
House disappears ~20 years ago (about the same age of [[Vinmyr]]. COINCIDENCE?!?!?!)

## [[Caldrik]] - [[Bogentle]]

Lived 40p years ago, and the son of a poor fisherman
Natural affinity to magic garnered him attention
Slew a black dragon, unsealed and plundered tombs, etc.
Spent time at his old retreat in [[Neverwinter]] woods, the old owl well
While there he studied lesser known magic such as [[Dunamancy]]
His book was his final magical work

## [[Morgan]] - Warlock Patron

Savors, the lord of divination, the all seeing, he of the third eye
Used to be the god of all spellcasters,  it was defeated and usurped Azuth, god of casters
Appears as a man with crystalline face and features, with a symbol of a crystal ball with eyes
The order of the third eye served him and would carve staffs that he would occupy
Velsharoon is a rival god of necromancy
Some of the main directives is to monitor and reduce the use of high level magic and lead followers to artifacts and lore
The shrine near Coneyberry is to Savros
The book does not mention what happened to the order of the third eye, just that membership declined with his defeat

## [[Doe]] - Phoenixes

Powerful elemental bird
Gargantuan in size 40 ft tall
Not evil, but by their nature are sometimes seen as evil by mortals
The feathers have many magical properties and uses, but must be harvested while alive
They also lose magic if the phoenix they were taken from die
Realm of fire where they reside is theoretically infinite
[[Kossuth]] is the lord of the plane of fire

## [[Vinmyr]] - Mention of the god of house [[Lumaday]]

Obsession of [[Dunamancy]] becomes personified in a being called the [[Luxon]]
Some say it predates all known gods
Mention artifacts called beacons, objects made of an intricate dark metal in the shape of a dodecahedron
Followers believe these beacons were the body of the [[Luxon]], and if they could be gathered and combined, perhaps he could reign supreme again
They heighten the power of casters, but even more so a [[Dunamancy|Dunamancer]]

## [[Caldrik]] - [[Dunamancy]]

Chronomancers can rewrite slow rewind time
Graviturges can manipulate gravity
Echo Knights are able to pull another version of themselves from another timeline to assist in combat
Speculatively connected to the creature known as the [[Luxon]]
The greatest number of followers come from the underdark, and are primarily drow
Also the best place to find beacons

## [[Morgan]] - the [[Harpers]]

A semi organized group
A coalition of individuals across the continent, focused on maintaining peace and knowledge and defending the weak
Work to counter groups like the [[Zhentarim]]
A major accomplishment included thwarting the cult of the dragon from summoning Tiamat
They seemingly always have a hand in things, but not at the forefront
They do good things and actual good
