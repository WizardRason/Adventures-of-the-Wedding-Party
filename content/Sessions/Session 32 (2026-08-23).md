A spiral staircase going down, and enter a chamber with beams of light coming down three of four tunnels. The party travels down the one with a flickering red light.

While traveling down the tunnel, the [[Wedding Party|Party]] sees a vision of three elves together in mirth. They have powers of creation, one crying tears of mirth that turned into many kinds of elves and one, a drow woman, creates a spider covered in vines and flowers. When the vision faded, the location changed to a room with a lava pool and a large stone ziggurat rising from the center. At the peak is a fire elemental and a cracked elemental gem.

After defeating the elemental, and charging the gem with some magic, the gem is repaired. The room is the filled with go.den fire like spider threads. The beam coming from here is no longer pulsing. The [[Wedding Party|Party]] returns to the central room.

When traveling down the water tunnel, another vision appears. There is conflict between the elves, and the drow woman grows envious of the other. She tries to strike him down, but fails and is locked away in darkness, twisted into the image of her making. The water gem we previously got was placed there and returned.

When traveling down the earth tunnel, another vision. The spider is cast out and transformed into a traditional spider form by the remaining elves. It is now full of hatred, and found others of its kind in a swamp like area.
Inside of the earth chamber is an earth elemental. The elemental is destroyed and the gem restored with magic. Returning to the main chamber.

Going down the air tunnel, we see an air view of the continent, with many places with similar sanctuaries. Also of spiders entering basins of ichor, and finally the first spider striking down the three elves, and finally being restored to his original glory.

The [[Wedding Party|Party]] is now welcome at the Silken Sanctuary, as we are now considered hero's for restoring the place. Most go down to watch spiders perform the plunge into the black ichor.
