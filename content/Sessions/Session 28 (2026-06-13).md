The [[Wedding Party|Party]] is faced with the insurmountable obstacle of the 10 foot gap

All the party is able to get past the gap

On the other side is a bucket to lower people and objects down, and [[Caldrik]] makes his way down. A giant worm snuck up to him, paralyzing him. It began to drag him away. The rest of us got down as quick as possible and saved him. It ran away after taking damage.

There are also kobalds that were heard down the other route, and we began heading in that direction. As we continue down, past some traps, we reach a larger room with for kobalds. They seem willing to negotiate. The leader seems very well spoken, and at least somewhat genuine, though hiding something.

The leader introduced himself as [[Kobold Clan|Trex]], and the others as [[Kobold Clan|Scorp]], [[Kobold Clan|Thwip]], and [[Kobold Clan|Smol]]. We continue talking, and he describes the remainder of the mines.

After conversation, we come to a bit of an agreement, and decide to vouch for them to [[Gundren]] in exchange for helping with the mine. They plan to head back up partially while we continue searching the mine.

With continued movement, we find a roper hanging on the wall. It surprised us and grabbed multiple characters, and knocked [[Morgan]] unconscious. [[Doe]] was able to bring her back up. The fight goes back and forth, and all we're able to escape from its grasp. With some effort, the roper is detached from the wall and falls to its demise. Searching on the other side of the rope, we find goggles of dark vision for [[Doe]].

We check on the rats when we get to the top of the mine, and began returning to [[Phandalin]] with the kobolds.
