We take the kobolds to the manor for safety for them after arriving back in [[Phandalin]]
[[Doe]] goes to the shrine of luck to be there in silence, and [[Morgan]] follows to keep an eye on him
[[Doe]] is approached by [[Buck]] in owl form, and they have a talk, about what they experienced in the world. After walking and talking, they eventually return.

[[Caldrik]] in the late night decides to look at his journal some more, see if there are any new writings, and reply to the ones there. He speaks to them, the self proclaimed enemy of his enemy. That _she_ has a hold on his heart, and that he can help, but first whoever he is must be freed. He must go to the Silken Sanctuary for more information.

The inn is mostly empty, and most people are bustling about for the election in the town, socializing and such. All the people out, conversations are had with [[Sildar]], [[Quelline]], and [[Sister Garaele]]. [[Vinmyr]] plans to sneak into the Miner's Exchange to get dirt on [[Halia]].

Speaking to [[Sister Garaele]], her thoughts on [[Halia]] is things will be ok for now. She also had some thoughts on what could affect [[Kobold Clan|Trex]] and increase his intellect. Possibly divine or possession, or maybe another form of magic. It's good to have some doubt.

[[Vinmyr]] looks to the Miners Exchange and attempts to find information or secret doors. He has bad luck and triggers multiple traps. He did steal some documents, some guards entered the room, and he escaped. One of the documents is a letter addressed to someone named Viper, with information almost confirming it was written by [[Halia]].

The results of the election are announced, and the winner is [[Toblen]]! He is surprised and begins giving his acceptance speech. While speaking though, with the rain, the [[Cryovain|White Dragon]] from the nearby mountain flies in, and swipes the stage with its tail. [[Halia]], [[Sildar]], and [[Toblen]] are knocked aside and not moving. The dragon floats above the crowd, and asks for [[Wedelia|Wedelia Buckshot]]. She walks forward, identifying herself. "[[Silas Grimm]] sends his regards", and a frozen breath comes down on her, almost debilitating her completely.

[[Caldrik]] and [[Morgan]] move to help the people struck down, and the rest of the [[Wedding Party|Party]] prepare for battle. A subset of people from the town step up and prepare to fight alongside against this guy.

[[Wedelia]] goes down, the town that we're not touched flee out of the square, and the rest of them take a stand around the Shrine of Luck. [[Doe]] heals her back up to at least be conscious. Goes down again.

[[Sildar]] is breathing and healed a little, [[Halia]] and [[Toblen]] are not. They get transported towards where [[Sister Garaele]] is. [[Cryovain]] left with the arrival of the [[Lords' Alliance]] troops, taking two townsfolk, including [[Trilena|Trilena Stonehill]].
